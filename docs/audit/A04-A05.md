# A04 Garage and A05 source evidence remediation

This ledger records implementation against the supplied A01–A11/B12 audit
package. Tests use invented readings, temporary databases and isolated local
servers. No household configuration, provider account or equipment was accessed.
Only the read-only v0.7.5 `easee.csv` and `st-mq.csv` import boundary retains old
ST-MQ compatibility. Current restoration obligations and deterministic replay
remain supported.

## Deliberately retained design

- Separate front/rear thermal reserves, recovery debt, bounded OFF permission and
  persistence-failure withdrawal remain; future charger heat is never credited
  as frost protection. This remains an OFF-opportunity model with distinct
  thermal/electrical readiness, not a Home preheat model.
- Ordinary native OFF and explicit owner Normal are distinct. External room
  temperature control retains its native 17 °C baseline and clear-before-handover
  contract; no i-save behavior is inferred.
- Genuine unchanged reports advance coverage without duplicating every value
  row. Cached/retained/out-of-order/contradictory/future observations cannot renew
  valid evidence. Missing required weighted room membership stays unknown.
- Interval-zero policy takes effect after the next genuine report. Changing a
  setting or reconnecting does not create a measurement or extend its deadline.
- Forecasts remain forecasts, unknown issue times remain unknown, missing solar
  remains missing, and previous-hour radiation alignment remains explicit.
- Sensor change/revert remains append-only and current reconstruction keeps
  frozen forecasts separate from corrected assessments.
